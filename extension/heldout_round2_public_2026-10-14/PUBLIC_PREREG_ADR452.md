**2. Scoring (fixed before the cases are read).**
*Mapping step (before any evaluator runs on a faulted workspace):* the development agent reads the source of the evaluators (as in ADR-060) and, for each case, writes a mapping from the case's assumed file layout to the layout the real evaluator reads, keeping the fault's mechanism. A case for which no mapping keeps the mechanism, or whose baseline does not pass the target check, is INCONCLUSIVE and is recorded as such at this step, with the reason and the artifacts, before any faulted run. Inconclusive may not be assigned after a faulted run because the check did not fire. The mapping files are hashed before the faulted runs.
*DETECTED:* the target check, which is the declared target in the case file, returns a failure on the faulted workspace that it does not return on the clean baseline, and the gate state meets the declared severity (critical: HALT; high or medium: ESCALATE or HALT). A target-check failure with a state below the declared severity is DETECTED and also recorded as a severity mismatch.
*MISSED:* the target check is reached on a valid case and does not fail, including a check declared in the contract that has no branch in the evaluator (as H02), and including the case in which another check fires.
*INCONCLUSIVE:* as above, and also a vacuous injection (the fault is not present after `apply_fault`) or an environmental incompatibility that prevents the evaluator from running.
*Denominator:* `eligible_n = 7 − inconclusive_n`; the proportion is `detected_n / eligible_n` with the Wilson 95% interval; inconclusive cases are in neither numerator nor denominator and are named with their reasons. If `eligible_n` is below 5 the counts are reported and no proportion is put in the abstract or a headline. Secondary descriptive outcome, reported beside and never instead: whether any check of the gate, or of any gate, stopped the faulted workspace (any-check interception).
*Re-run:* one re-run is allowed, only for a defect in the mapping or the evaluation script (never in an evaluator, contract or threshold); both runs are reported.

What stands in place of independence: the cases were written blind by another AI that had seen none of the project; the evaluators are fixed by hash before the cases are read; the rules, the mapping rule and the prediction are fixed first; the first author supervises; every classification is archived with its evidence.
